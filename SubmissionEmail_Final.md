**Chapter title:**
A Systematic Review of IoT-Enabled Smart Agriculture for Food Security and Sustainable Development: Opportunities, Challenges, and Future Directions

**Authors:**
Dr. Sonia Ben Aissa and Prof. Mounir Frikha
Department of Computer Networks and Communications, College of Computer Sciences and Information Technology, King Faisal University, Al-Ahsa, Saudi Arabia

**Summary:**
The chapter presents a PRISMA 2020 systematic review synthesizing 65 peer-reviewed studies (2018–2025) on IoT applications in smart agriculture, with a dual-impact framework examining both positive contributions (water efficiency, crop yield, pest detection, livestock monitoring, climate adaptation) and negative externalities (e-waste, energy, cybersecurity, digital divide). The review focuses specifically on developing country contexts and proposes five priority research directions and a set of policy recommendations aligned with SDG 2, SDG 9, and SDG 13.
